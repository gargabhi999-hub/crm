Privacy Policy

Nexus CRM — Nexus Staff & Nexus Admin

Developer: UniVerseOrder / Nexus CRM • Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Apps Covered: Nexus Staff (com.nexuscrm.staff), Nexus Admin (com.nexuscrm.admin), and Nexus CRM Web Portal

This Privacy Policy describes how Nexus CRM ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when authorized team members, team leaders, and administrators use the Nexus Staff and Nexus Admin mobile applications and the Nexus CRM web application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Information: Work name, email address, username, company assignment, role (Agent, Team Leader, Admin, SuperAdmin), and salted Argon2id password hashes.
  • CRM & Workflow Data: Customer contact records uploaded or managed by your organization, call dispositions, appointments, callbacks, follow-up notes, payment/transaction references, and work/break session logs.
  • Camera & Media Files (Optional, User-Initiated): When a user chooses to capture or upload a payment receipt image for conversion confirmation, the app requests access to the device Camera or Photo Library solely to extract transaction details via OCR and attach the verification record.
  • Security & Session Metadata: Short-lived authentication tokens stored securely on-device via encrypted storage (Android Keystore / SecureStore), device network IP addresses for rate limiting, and Cloudflare Turnstile anti-abuse verification tokens during sign-in and password resets.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users, enforce role-based and company-isolated workspace access, and protect accounts against brute-force or automated attacks.
  • To synchronize leads, calling workflows, appointments, callbacks, notifications, receipts, and analytics reports across web and mobile clients in real time.
  • To send transactional emails requested by users or administrators (such as password reset links or payment confirmation receipts).

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell personal data and do not display third-party advertising. Operational data is processed only by infrastructure providers strictly required to deliver the service:

  • Cloud Hosting & PostgreSQL Database: Secure encrypted data storage and API hosting.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Bot and login abuse verification.
  • Email Delivery (SMTP / Brevo): Transactional password resets and payment confirmation receipts.
  • OCR Receipt Processing: Extracts transaction IDs/amounts only when a receipt screenshot is explicitly uploaded by an authenticated user.

4. Data Security

All communication between mobile/web clients and Nexus CRM servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, refresh tokens are stored as SHA-256 digests with rotation on use, and mobile session credentials are protected in encrypted hardware-backed device storage.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Workspace records are retained for as long as your organization's account remains active. Users and company administrators may request account deactivation or permanent deletion at any time using the instructions on our Account Deletion page.

6. Children's Privacy

Nexus Staff and Nexus Admin are intended exclusively for enterprise and workplace users aged 18 and older and are not directed to children under 13 (or applicable local age of consent).
