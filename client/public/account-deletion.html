Account & Data Deletion — Nexus CRM

Applies to Nexus Staff (com.nexuscrm.staff), Nexus Admin (com.nexuscrm.admin), and the Nexus CRM web portal.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Via Company Admin: An authorized Admin or SuperAdmin can deactivate or delete your user profile directly in the Users module inside Nexus Admin or the web dashboard.
  2. Via Email Request: Send an email from your registered work email address to lakshayb059@gmail.com with the subject Nexus CRM Account Deletion Request and include your registered username/email and company name.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted: User profile credentials, active refresh tokens, reset tokens, and personal login access are permanently revoked and purged within 7 business days of verification.
  • Company CRM Records: Business lead logs, receipts, and audit records belonging to your employer/organization remain under the company's data controller policy unless the company administrator requests full tenant erasure.
